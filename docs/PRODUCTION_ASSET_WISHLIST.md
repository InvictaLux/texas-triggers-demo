# Texas Triggers production asset wishlist

These are recommended future shoots or asset packages to make the production redesign stronger without fabricating product context.

## Flagship product macro set

- Diablo FRT installed-component macro photographs on a neutral graphite surface.
- Three-quarter, side, and detail views with consistent lighting and no overlaid text.
- Intended use: hero, product detail gallery, feature blocks, zoom views, and product cards.

## Platform-specific product photography

- Reaper FRT, TX22 FRT, Glock FRT, 1911/2011 FRT, SKS FRT, and Canik FRT photographed as physical products.
- One clean product-only image and one installed/context image per platform where appropriate.
- Intended use: platform selector cards, category pages, compatibility results, and SEO landing pages.

## Installation/workbench imagery

- Hands/tools/components at a clean workbench, photographed for documentation rather than advertising.
- Neutral close-ups showing fitment-sensitive parts without implying universal compatibility.
- Intended use: Installation Center, guide previews, component recommendations, and support articles.

## Packaging and fulfillment

- Box, included components, manuals, and shipping presentation for each flagship system.
- Intended use: product reassurance blocks, FAQ/support, and post-purchase onboarding.

## Lifestyle and range imagery

- Texas Triggers team or customer range imagery with signed releases and clear product context.
- Dealer/store imagery for the wholesale/dealer experience.
- Intended use: company credibility, dealer section, reviews, and curated community content.

## Video capture

- Consistent branded thumbnails for each installation video.
- Short product-detail clips for gallery/video support.
- Intended use: Installation Center cards, product galleries, and platform landing pages.
