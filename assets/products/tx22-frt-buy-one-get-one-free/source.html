Pre-Orders & Backorders — Cancellation

Orders for pre-order or backordered items may be cancelled for a full refund, provided the order has not yet entered the shipping and fulfillment process. Once an order has been processed for shipment, it is no longer eligible for cancellation and must follow the standard return process below.

Standard Order Cancellations

To request a cancellation, contact us immediately at info@texastriggerusa.com with your order number.

Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, all of the following conditions must be met:

  • The item must be in new, unused, and uninstalled condition.
  • Original packaging must be completely intact and unopened — items with opened packaging are not eligible for return.
  • For apparel and merchandise: all original tags must be attached and the item must be unworn and unused.
  • All original accessories, manuals, and components must be included.
  • You must contact us within 5 days of confirmed delivery to initiate a return.
  • You must ship the item back to us within 7 days of receiving return approval.
Non-Returnable Items

The following items are strictly non-returnable under any circumstances:

  • Any product that has been installed, used, fired, or assembled into a firearm.
  • Any product with opened or damaged packaging.
  • Apparel or merchandise with removed tags or signs of use.
  • Items returned beyond the 5-day contact window or 7-day return shipment window.
  • Pre-owned, used, or previously installed components.
Failed Inspection — Abandoned Items

If a returned item is received and fails inspection due to installation, use, damage, or non-compliance with our return policy, Texas Triggers reserves the right to deny the return and withhold the refund. Return Shipping

Exchange Policy

Refund Processing
